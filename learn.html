Professional learning · 15–25 minutes

Learn to see the affordances.

A space is not automatically better than a classroom. Learn to identify when a different environment offers meaningful opportunities for learning.

1 · Understand the SPACE framework

Our diagnostic dimensions are adapted here as prompts for lesson planning. This teacher-facing application is a proposed adaptation, not a validated lesson-design instrument.

2 · Explore Zhonghua's spaces

Choose a real school space. Look carefully at its features, then consider what students could do there that would be difficult or less authentic in class.

3 · Would you leave the classroom?

P3 Science: Students compare observable characteristics of plants. The classroom has clear photographs and specimens. The Eco-garden offers living plants in their environment.

4 · Turn observation into design

Relocated lesson

Students sit at the garden and complete a worksheet using photographs of plants.

Affordance-driven lesson

Students observe living plants, collect evidence of variation, compare observations and justify grouping decisions.
